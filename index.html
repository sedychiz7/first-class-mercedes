03 · Computer diagnostics

We plug in.The car talks.

We connect your car to Xentry, the diagnostic system built for Mercedes-Benz. It talks to every control unit, reads stored and live faults, and shows what each sensor is reporting in real time.

That means no guesswork and no replacing parts "just to see". You get a clear picture of what's wrong and what it will take to fix it.
